Cookie Policy

Last updated: 2 October 2026

1. Short version

This website does not use cookies, and does not store anything on your device (no local storage, session storage or similar). That is why you are not asked to accept a cookie banner.

2. What this means in practice

  • No analytics or statistics tools (such as Google Analytics).
  • No advertising, retargeting or social media tracking pixels (such as Meta Pixel).
  • No embedded third-party content (videos, maps or social feeds) that could set its own cookies.
  • Fonts and scripts are served from this website itself, not from third-party networks.

3. When you leave this site

Links to client websites and to social media (Facebook, Instagram, LinkedIn, YouTube, TikTok and X) take you to other services. Those services may set their own cookies under their own policies once you visit them.

4. Contact form

Sending the contact form passes your message to FormSubmit, which delivers it to my inbox. This site does not set a cookie when you do this. See the Privacy Policy for how that data is handled.

5. Changes

If cookies or similar technologies are ever added, this page will be updated first, and you will be asked for consent before any non-essential cookie is set.
